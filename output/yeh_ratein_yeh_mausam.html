▶

Yeh Ratein Yeh Mausam

This song describes a romantic setting by a riverbank under the moonlight, where two lovers express their commitment to stay together forever. It captures the joy and deep affection between them amidst a beautiful, enchanting natural backdrop.

Movie / Album Dilli Ka Thug (1958)
Singers Asha Bhosle, Kishore Kumar
Actors
NutanKishore Kumar
Composer O.P. Nayyar
Lyricist Majrooh Sultanpuri
Source Shemaroo Filmi Gaane Unknown
