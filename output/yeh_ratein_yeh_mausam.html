▶

Yeh Ratein Yeh Mausam

This is a classic romantic duet set against the backdrop of a beautiful night by a river. The song expresses the joy and unwavering commitment of two lovers who promise to stay together forever, celebrating their profound connection under the enchanting moonlight and stars.

Movie / Album Dilli Ka Thug (1958)
Singers Asha Bhosle, Kishore Kumar
Actors
NutanKishore Kumar
Composer O. P. Nayyar
Lyricist Majrooh Sultanpuri

Song Structure & Translation

Chorus 1
Yeh raatein yeh mausam, nadee ka kinaara, yeh chanchal hawa
ये रातें ये मौसम, नदी का किनारा, ये चंचल हवा
These nights, these seasons, the river bank, this playful breeze,
Kaha do dilon ne, ke milkar kabhi hum na honge judaa
कहा दो दिलों ने, कि मिलकर कभी हम न होंगे जुदा
Two hearts have said that, meeting, we will never be separated.
↳ Yeh raatein yeh mausam, nadee ka kinaara, yeh chanchal hawa ↺ Repeated
Verse 1
Yeh kya baat hai aaj ki chandni mein
ये क्या बात है आज की चाँदनी में
What is this special thing in today's moonlight?
Ke hum kho gaye pyaar ki raagini mein
कि हम खो गए प्यार की रागिनी में
That we are lost in the melody of love.
Sunaa rahi hai fiza, geet koi
सुना रही है फ़िज़ा, गीत कोई
The atmosphere is singing some song,
Jise sunke jaage hain armaan kai
जिसे सुनके जागे हैं अरमान कई
Hearing which, many desires have awakened.
Chorus 2

↳ Yeh raatein yeh mausam, nadee ka kinaara, yeh chanchal hawa ↺ Repeated
Verse 2
Sitaron ki mehfil ne karke ishaara
सितारों की महफ़िल ने करके इशारा
The gathering of stars, by making a sign,
Kaha ab to saara jahaan hai tumhara
कहा अब तो सारा जहाँ है तुम्हारा
Said, "Now the whole world is yours."
Mohabbat jawan ho, khula aasmaan ho
मोहब्बत जवाँ हो, खुला आसमान हो
May love be young, may the sky be open,
Kare koi dil aarzoo aur kya
करे कोई दिल आरज़ू और क्या
What more could any heart desire?
Chorus 3
↳ Kaha do dilon ne, ke milkar kabhi hum na honge judaa ↺ Repeated

Verse 3
Kasam hai tumhe tum agar mujhse roothe
कसम है तुम्हें तुम अगर मुझसे रूठे
I swear to you, if you get upset with me,
Rahe saans jab tak yeh bandhan na toote
रहे साँस जब तक ये बंधन न टूटे
May this bond not break as long as there is breath.
Tumhe dil diya hai, yeh waada kiya hai
तुम्हें दिल दिया है, ये वादा किया है
I have given you my heart, I have made this promise,
Sanam main tumhari rahungi sada
सनम मैं तुम्हारी रहूँगी सदा
My beloved, I will always be yours.
