Kora Kagaz Tha Yeh Man Mera

This song expresses the profound transformation a person's life undergoes when they fall in love. The heart, once a blank slate, is now filled with the beloved's name and presence, bringing joy and meaning to a previously desolate existence.

Movie / Album Aradhana (1969)
Singers Lata Mangeshkar, Kishore Kumar
Actors
Rajesh KhannaSharmila Tagore
Composer S.D. Burman
Lyricist Anand Bakshi
Source Saregama Music 4K

Vocabulary Guide

Kora (कोरा)
Koh-raa
Blank, empty, untouched. Refers to something pristine or unused.
Kagaz (कागज़)
Kaa-gaz
Paper. In this context, it symbolizes the mind or heart as a surface for experiences.
Man (मन)
Mun
Mind, heart, soul, inner self. Often used to denote one's feelings and thoughts.
Likh diya (लिख दिया)
Likh dee-yaa
Wrote (past tense). Implies an action of inscribing or marking permanently.
Tera (तेरा)
Teh-raa
Your (informal, singular). Used when addressing a close friend or beloved.
Soona (सूना)
Soo-naa
Desolate, empty, quiet, lonely. Describes a place or state lacking vibrancy.
Aangan (आँगन)
Aan-gun
Courtyard, compound. The open space in front or within a house, often a place of gathering.
Jeevan (जीवन)
Jee-vun
Life. The state of being alive; existence.
Bas gaya (बस गया)
Bus guh-yaa
Settled, resided, made a home. Implies a permanent or deep establishment.
Pyar (प्यार)
Pyaa-r
Love. A strong feeling of affection.
Toot na jaaye (टूटना जाए)
Too-t naa jaa-yeh
May not break, lest it breaks. Expresses a fear or concern about something being shattered.
Sapne (सपने)
Sup-neh
Dreams (plural of sapna). Aspirations, visions, or images experienced during sleep.
Darta hoon (डरता हूँ)
Dur-taa hoo
I fear, I am afraid. Expresses a state of apprehension.
Din rain (दिन रैन)
Din rai-n
Day and night. Continuously, constantly.
Socha karta hoon (सोचा करता हूँ)
Soh-chaa kur-taa hoo
I keep thinking, I used to think. Implies a continuous or habitual thought process.
Nainon (नैनों)
Nai-no
Eyes (poetic plural of 'nain'). Often used in poetry for a more lyrical feel.
Basa hai (बसा है)
Bus-aa hai
Has resided, has settled. Similar to 'bas gaya' but in a continuous present perfect tense.
Khoya khoya sa (खोया खोया सा)
Kho-yaa kho-yaa saa
Lost, absent-minded, dreamy. Describes a state of being engrossed or distracted by thoughts.
Rehta hoon (रहता हूँ)
Reh-taa hoo
I remain, I stay, I live. Indicates a continuous state or action.
Zindagi (ज़िंदगी)
Zin-duh-gee
Life. The general term for existence.
Khushi (ख़ुशी)
Khoo-shee
Happiness, joy. A state of well-being and contentment.
Roop (रूप)
Roo-p
Beauty, form, appearance. Often refers to physical attractiveness.
Rani (रानी)
Raa-nee
Queen. A female monarch or a term of endearment for a beloved woman.
Saagar (सागर)
Saa-gur
Ocean, sea. Metaphorically, a vast quantity or source of something.
Reh paoon (रह पाऊँ)
Reh paa-oo
Be able to live/stay. Expresses capability or possibility of continuing to exist or remain.
Saathiya (साथिया)
Saa-thee-yaa
Companion, beloved, partner. An affectionate term for someone close.
Jeena (जीना)
Jee-naa
To live, living. The act of existing or experiencing life.
