Kora Kagaz Tha Yeh Man Mera

This romantic song beautifully captures the essence of a heart that was once empty and pure, like a blank paper, now being filled with the colors and stories of love by a beloved. It expresses the joy and fear associated with new love, and the desire to live and die with the cherished one.

Movie / Album Aradhana (1969)
Singers Lata Mangeshkar, Kishore Kumar
Actors
Rajesh KhannaSharmila Tagore
Composer S.D. Burman
Lyricist Anand Bakshi

Vocabulary Guide

Kora koh-raa
Blank, empty, untouched, pure.
Kagaz kaa-gaz
Paper.
Man mun
Heart, mind, soul, inner self. Often used to refer to one's emotional core.
Likh diya likh dee-yaa
Wrote, inscribed. 'Likhna' means to write, 'diya' is the past participle of 'dena' (to give), here used as an auxiliary verb to indicate completion.
Tera teh-raa
Yours (informal singular).
Soona soo-naa
Empty, desolate, deserted, lonely.
Aangan aan-gun
Courtyard, patio, the open space in front or center of a house.
Jeevan jee-vun
Life.
Bas gayaa bus guh-yaa
Settled, resided, became established, filled up. 'Basna' means to settle or dwell.
Pyaar pyaa-r
Love.
Toot na jaaye too-t naa jaa-ye
May not break, shatter, or be destroyed. Expresses a fear or wish against something breaking.
Sapne sup-ne
Dreams (plural of 'sapna').
Darta hoon dur-taa hooñ
I fear, I am afraid. 'Darna' means to fear.
Din raat din raa-t
Day and night, continuously.
Jeeta hoon jee-taa hooñ
I live. 'Jeena' means to live.
Jab se jub seh
Since, ever since.
Mili hai mi-lee hai
Has met, has been found (feminine singular). 'Milna' means to meet or find.
Nazar nuh-zur
Gaze, sight, glance, vision.
Laga hai luh-gaa hai
Has begun, has started, has been applied. 'Lagana' means to apply, to start.
Khushiyon khu-shee-yoñ
Of happiness (genitive plural of 'khushi').
Safar suh-fur
Journey, travel.
Kya kahoon kyaa kuh-hooñ
What should I say?
Na kahoon naa kuh-hooñ
Not say.
Bas itna hi bus it-naa hee
Just this much, only this.
Kehna hai keh-naa hai
Have to say, is to be said. 'Kehna' means to say.
Milkar mil-kur
After meeting, together (gerund of 'milna').
Marna hai mur-naa hai
Have to die, is to die. 'Marna' means to die.
