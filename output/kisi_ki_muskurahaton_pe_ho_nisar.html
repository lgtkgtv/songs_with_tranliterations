Kisi Ki Muskurahaton Pe Ho Nisar

This song advocates a philosophy of selfless living, emphasizing finding joy in the happiness of others and dedicating one's life to humanity. It encourages compassion, kindness, and enduring personal struggles for the greater good, defining a meaningful life through altruism.

Movie / Album Anari (1959)
Singers Mukesh
Actors
Raj Kapoor
Composer Shankar-Jaikishan
Lyricist Shailendra

Song Structure & Translation

Chorus 1
Kisi ki muskurahaton pe ho nisar
किसी की मुस्कुराहटों पे हो निसार
May you sacrifice yourself for someone's smiles,
Kisi ka dard mil sake to le udhar
किसी का दर्द मिल सके तो ले उधार
If you can get someone's pain, take it as a loan.
Kisi ke vaste ho tere dil mein pyar
किसी के वास्ते हो तेरे दिल में प्यार
May there be love in your heart for someone,
Jeena isi ka naam hai
जीना इसी का नाम है
This is what living is called.
Verse 1
Mana apni jeb se fakeer hain
माना अपनी जेब से फ़कीर हैं
Agreed, we are poor from our pockets,
Phir bhi yaron dil ke hum ameer hain
फिर भी यारों दिल के हम अमीर हैं
Still, friends, we are rich at heart.
Mite jo pyar ke liye woh zindagi
मिटे जो प्यार के लिए वो ज़िंदगी
That life which is erased for love,
Jale jo aman ke liye woh roshni
जले जो अमन के लिए वो रोशनी
That light which burns for peace.

Vocabulary Guide

Nisar ni-saar
To sacrifice, to dedicate completely; to be devoted to.
Udhar oo-dhaar
Loan, credit; here, metaphorically 'to take on' or 'borrow' someone's pain.
Vaste vaas-te
For the sake of, for; on behalf of.
Fakeer fa-keer
A mendicant, a poor person; often used metaphorically for someone lacking material wealth but rich in spirit.
Ameer a-meer
Rich, wealthy; here, 'rich at heart'.
Mite mi-te
To be erased, to be destroyed, to end; here, 'that which is dedicated' or 'sacrificed'.
Aman a-man
Peace, tranquility.
Roshni rosh-nee
Light, brightness.
Anmol an-mol
Priceless, invaluable, precious.
Khoon ke rishte khoon ke rish-te
Blood relations, family ties.
Pi gaya pee ga-ya
Drank; here, metaphorically 'endured' or 'swallowed' (tears/sorrow).
Gham gham
Sorrow, grief, sadness.
Seh gaya seh ga-ya
Endured, tolerated, bore (pain or difficulty).
