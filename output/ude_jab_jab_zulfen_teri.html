Ude Jab Jab Zulfen Teri

A playful and romantic song where the male protagonist expresses his admiration for the female protagonist's beauty and charm, particularly how her flowing hair captivates him and makes his heart flutter, while she teases him back, acknowledging her allure.

Movie / Album Naya Daur (1957)
Singers Mohammed Rafi, Asha Bhosle
Actors
VyjayantimalaDilip Kumar
Composer O.P. Nayyar
Lyricist Sahir Ludhianvi
Source Shemaroo Filmi Gaane HD

Vocabulary Guide

Ude
Oo-day
To fly, to flutter, to rise (here, referring to hair flying in the wind).
Zulfen
Zool-fen
Tresses, locks of hair, especially long, flowing hair.
Kawaariyon
Ka-waa-ree-yon
Of bachelors, unmarried young men (plural genitive case).
Machle
Much-lay
To flutter, to be restless, to yearn, to be agitated with desire.
Chikne
Chik-nay
Smooth, glossy. In this context, it implies attractive, charming, or alluring.
Chehre
Cheh-ray
Faces (plural).
Nazar phisle
Na-zar phis-lay
Gaze slips, to be attracted or captivated by someone's appearance.
Peechhe pade hain
Pee-chhay pa-day hain
Are after, pursuing, following (often romantically).
Ladke
Lad-kay
Boys, young men (plural).
Taraf se
Ta-raf say
On behalf of, from the side of.
Akela
A-kay-la
Alone, solitary.
Haseena
Ha-see-na
A beautiful woman, a beauty.
Kameenah
Ka-mee-na
Rascal, scoundrel, naughty one. Used playfully here, not as a serious insult.
Dekhke
Dekh-kay
By seeing, upon seeing (gerund form of 'dekhna' - to see).
Dhadakta hai
Dha-dak-ta hai
Beats, throbs (referring to the heart).
Roop
Roop
Beauty, form, appearance, charm.
Jaadu
Jaa-doo
Magic, spell, enchantment.
Chalta hai
Chal-ta hai
Works, operates, runs (here, referring to magic working its effect).
