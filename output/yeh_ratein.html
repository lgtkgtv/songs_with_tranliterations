English Translation
Yeh ratein, yeh mausam, nadi ka kinara, yeh chanchal hawa
ये रातें, ये मौसम, नदी का किनारा, ये चंचल हवा
These nights, this weather, the riverbank, this playful breeze.
Kaha do dilon ne, ki milkar kabhi hum na honge juda
कहा दो दिलों ने, कि मिलकर कभी हम ना होंगे जुदा
Two hearts have declared that, having met, we shall never part.
